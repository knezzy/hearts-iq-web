Common questions

How do I win at Hearts?

Keep your score as low as you can. Hearts are worth one point each and the Queen of Spades is worth thirteen. Follow suit when possible and pay attention to which cards have already been played.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Play mode works against computer opponents. Game Center features, feedback, and other online services need a connection.

Where are the leaderboards?

Hearts IQ uses Apple's Game Center. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings, then open the game's rankings. If a leaderboard does not load, check your connection and try again.

How can I send feedback or ask about my data?

Email us using the address on this page. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description when reporting a technical issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Need a hand?

For gameplay, settings, technical issues, or privacy requests, email Hearts IQ support.
