Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use the Hearts IQ app and website.

Last updated October 28, 2026

This policy describes how Hearts IQ, published by Kyle Knez, handles information in the iOS app and on this website.

Information we handle

  • Gameplay and settings: Progress, statistics, and preferences are stored on your device. If you use Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center identity and leaderboard submissions.
  • Feedback: If you choose to send feedback through the app, we receive your message, an anonymous Firebase user identifier, the source of the message, app version, build number, and whether the app is a debug, TestFlight, or production build. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include.
  • Analytics: Production builds use Firebase Analytics to record app use and gameplay events, such as mode opened, hand completed, and game completed. Firebase may process an app instance identifier and device or app information needed to provide analytics. Debug and TestFlight builds disable Analytics collection in the app.
  • App services and protection: Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier if you send feedback through the app. Remote Config processes technical app and device information, including country, language, time zone, and OS version. Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest on supported devices to help verify Firebase requests.

Why we use it

We use this information to run the game and leaderboards, respond to feedback, understand how the app performs, and protect Firebase services from abuse. We do not use Hearts IQ feedback to target ads. The current app edition has no ads enabled.

Who receives it

Google Firebase processes feedback, authentication, analytics, remote configuration, and app verification for us. Apple processes Game Center data when you use Game Center. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and choices

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you remove it or the app. Feedback and related anonymous identifiers are retained as needed for support and abuse prevention. You can ask us to review or delete feedback associated with your anonymous account; include enough detail about the message to help us locate it. You can manage Game Center through Apple.

This website

This static website has no analytics, ads, account forms, or tracking scripts of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email kyleknez4@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app's features or data handling change.
